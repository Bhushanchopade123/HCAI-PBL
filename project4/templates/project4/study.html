Choose Your Study Design

Please select one of the two preference elicitation methods below. Both will learn your movie preferences and recommend films you might enjoy.

Design 1 — Pairwise Comparison

You will be shown 2 movies at a time and asked to pick which one you would rather watch. This repeats 10 times.

Simple and quick — one decision at a time.

Design 2 — Ranking

You will be shown 10 movies at a time and asked to drag and drop them into your preferred order. This repeats 3 times.

More information per round — gives richer preference data.
